      What it checks

      • Unmet load hours

        Proposed and every baseline against the 300-hour limit, counted from the timestep data, with the zones and hours that drive it.

      • Simulation health

        Severe errors, warnings that change results, and a plain-language fix for each common .err message.

      • Baseline and proposed match

        Schedules, internal loads, outdoor air, infiltration, areas, design days and weather file that must be the same in both.

      • Baseline rules

        System type, fan power, efficiencies, envelope, window-to-wall ratio and the four rotations, for 90.1-2010 and 2016.

      What it doesn't do

      It reads results you have already simulated; it does not run EnergyPlus (the command-line version can). It covers the checks that can be made from the outputs and the IDF, not drawings or the energy cost budget, and it can be wrong: treat a FAIL as a strong hint and a REVIEW as something to look at, and keep your own judgement.

      Where the rules come from

      ASHRAE 90.1-2010 and 2016 Appendix G as used by LEED v4 and v4.1, and the review comments energy modellers keep receiving. Each rule was tested by planting the mistake in a model and confirming it is caught, with no false alarms on the clean models; the scorecards are in the repository.
