Window-to-wall ratio extractor for Rhino

A fault-tolerant geometry parser that reads per-floor window-to-wall ratios straight from 3D thermal massing models and exports simulation-ready CSV.

Highlights

  • Treats the model as a data structure, not a drawing
  • Survives slab holes, micro-gaps and multi-storey zones
  • Distributes multi-floor glazing by vertical overlap

Window-to-wall ratio is a basic input to every energy model and green-building submission, and measuring it by hand is slow. Architectural models are built to look right, not to be measured: slabs have holes for cores and shafts, thermal zones leave 1 to 10 mm gaps that break Boolean unions, and atriums or curtain walls span several floors.

How it works

The script scans layers automatically, separating thermal zones, underground zones and glazing. It sorts zones by elevation with a 1.5 m tolerance and ignores artefacts under 2 m to find the true floors. For each floor it slices the massing at 1.2 m above the slab, unions the section curves with a 0.1 m tolerance and keeps the largest outer ring as the facade perimeter. Glazing that spans floors is split by how much of its bounding box overlaps each storey. Redraw is switched off while it runs, and the result is saved as a per-floor CSV.
