Remote sensing for environmental monitoring

Open satellite data and Google Earth Engine used to track air and water quality along an industrial coastline.

Location
Taiwan's west coast
Area
400,000 ha
Period studied
2020 to 2023
Tools
Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2, Sentinel-5P

Highlights

  • No field survey required
  • One script reruns for any date range
  • NO₂ from Sentinel-5P, river turbidity from Sentinel-2

I grew up on Taiwan's west coast, where rivers and farmland sit beside dense industry. Air and water quality are long-running concerns there, yet monitoring still relies on field surveys and consultant simulations that are slow and expensive. This independent project tests a cheaper, repeatable alternative built on open satellite data.

Air

Nitrogen dioxide is a key marker of industrial and traffic emissions. I filtered Copernicus Sentinel-5P NO₂ column density in Google Earth Engine, averaged it by year and clipped it to the study area to compare 2020 through 2023.

Water

Turbidity stands in for river health. From Sentinel-2 imagery I built a water mask with NDWI (green and near-infrared bands, thresholded, converted to polygons and dissolved), then applied NDTI (red and green bands) to water pixels only. The date range is a parameter, so the same script produces any year for comparison.
