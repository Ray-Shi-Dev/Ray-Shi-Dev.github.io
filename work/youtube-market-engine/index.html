YouTube market intelligence engine

A Streamlit data product that pulls YouTube data in parallel, isolates videos that beat their own channel's baseline and scores how open a niche is from 0 to 100.

Highlights

  • Parallel API ingestion across channels
  • Viral multiplier: views over the channel's median
  • Niche score with a penalty for markets dominated by large channels

Creators usually pick topics by instinct, which fails in crowded markets. This tool measures the barrier to entry for a topic with live data, so a high-demand, low-competition niche stands out and a market held by a few giant channels is flagged.

How it works

For a topic and region it finds the top channels, then fetches their upload playlists and statistics in parallel with a thread pool to cut ingestion time. Each video gets a viral multiplier, its views divided by the median views of its own channel, so demand is measured independently of subscriber count. The niche score rewards market depth (how many outliers exist) and viral intensity (how strong they are), and applies a penalty when channels with more than a million subscribers hold over half the market.

Interface

The Streamlit app has a single-topic dashboard with a score gauge, an opportunity map of upload date against viral multiplier, a tag explorer for long-tail keywords, and a head-to-head comparison of two topics.
