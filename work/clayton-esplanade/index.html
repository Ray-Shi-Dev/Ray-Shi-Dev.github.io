Selected work / Urban design

Clayton Esplanade masterplan

A canalfront mixed-use neighbourhood that brings health, work and recreation to an east Manchester suburb facing deprivation.

Location
Clayton, Manchester, UK
Area
22 ha
Context
MSc studio, The University of Manchester
Themes
Mixed-use blocks, CPTED, sustainable drainage

Highlights

  • A new service centre on the canalfront
  • Vertical and horizontal mixed-use block types
  • A road hierarchy that separates recreation from traffic

Clayton is a suburb in east Manchester. Following the Manchester Core Strategy and the Eastlands regeneration plans, this masterplan aims to reduce deprivation and raise quality of life by building a sustainable community around the canal.

Proposal

A new service centre on the canalfront gathers health, employment and leisure. Block types mix uses vertically and horizontally, and heights and density step to suit their surroundings. A public-space network serves visitors, residents and commuters, the road hierarchy gives the recreation corridor its own route, and the scheme applies crime prevention through environmental design (CPTED) and sustainable urban drainage (SuDS).
