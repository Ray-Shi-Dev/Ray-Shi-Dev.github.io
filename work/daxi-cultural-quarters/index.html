Daxi Cultural Quarters

A block design for a historic Japanese-era town that gives young residents places to live and work without erasing the street's character.

Location
Daxi, Taoyuan, Taiwan
Site area
430 m²
Context
Architecture design studio
Themes
Heritage, vertical public space, mixed communities

Highlights

  • Colonnaded walkways and building patterns studied block by block
  • Gehl's public-space theory translated into corridors, stairs and courtyards
  • Three building types for different communities

Daxi's old streets bring together everyday commerce on Central and Zhongzheng Roads, artisans on Zhongshan and Denglong Roads, and an ageing community. Over time these groups and uses drifted apart, and piecemeal change wore down the town's character.

Design

The project studies the colonnaded walkways and individual building patterns, then turns Gehl's public-space theory vertical: corridors, staircases and courtyards form a new shared network. Building masses are sorted into three types for different communities, with a care station, a convenience store and catering at street level. Sections and details resolve structural tree planting, drainage and daylight.
