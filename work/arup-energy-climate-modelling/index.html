At Arup I ran the environmental and energy simulations behind sustainable design decisions and policy-driven assessments: whole-building energy models, annual daylight studies, facade solar radiation for photovoltaics, and computational fluid dynamics.

Energy models

I automated model setup in Grasshopper. The scripts extrude floor plates into thermal zones, intersect them with facade and core masses, solve every surface's boundary condition, and generate windows from a target window-to-wall ratio before writing the model to .hbjson. In EnergyPlus I configured plant loops, HVAC systems, daylight controls and ASHRAE 90.1/62.1 schedules, then compared end-use breakdowns and optimisation strategies against baseline models.

Daylight and solar

LightStanza runs annual Radiance-based daylight simulations on models brought in from Revit or Rhino. Its outputs, including spatial daylight autonomy, annual sunlight exposure, illuminance grids and glare maps, support LEED v4 daylight credits. Facade radiation maps combined with PV module specifications estimate how much of a building's demand facade-mounted solar can offset.

Airflow

For CFD I used Butterfly and Ladybug in Grasshopper with OpenFOAM: set inlet, outlet and turbulence conditions, refine the mesh around facades and openings, and post-process velocity, pressure and streamlines in ParaView to find comfort zones and judge ventilation.
